The Most Famous World Cup Songs

The most famous World Cup song is Shakira's "Waka Waka," released in 2010, with 4.7 billion views on YouTube.

The Worst World Cup Song

*We Are One (Ole Ola)* is often considered the weakest World Cup song because it felt like a generic American pop track that lacked authentic Brazilian Samba rhythms. Furthermore, Brazilian artist Claudia Leitte was relegated to a tiny verse at the end, making local culture feel sidelined in favor of foreign pop stars. Coming right after Shakira's iconic *Waka Waka*, it simply failed to deliver the same genuine cultural energy and unity.
